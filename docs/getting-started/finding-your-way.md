# Finding your way

The app names its parts **Project › Flight › Dataset**: a project holds a site's flights, a flight holds the images
of one flight of the drone, and a dataset is a flight's images converted to temperatures, with its mosaics.

## The navigation pane

A pane on the left of every page lists the projects, under each its flights, under each its datasets, under each
dataset with frames its **Frames**, then **User Preferences**, **System Settings** and **Storage**. The pages' own
tabs and buttons go further: the viewer's tabs, Compare, the reports.

- A dataset opens its mosaics (else its frames, else its flight's Datasets tab), and is marked on any of its pages; a
  flight is marked on its Flight report.
- **Frames** opens the dataset's [frames explorer](../guide/frames.md), and is marked there.
- Projects, flights and datasets fold. What the pane unfolds to show the page shown stays unfolded until folded.
- The whole pane hides, by the header's menu button or the small tab on its edge. Over a narrow screen it opens on
  top of the page.
- The box under Projects filters the projects by name, and stays as typed from page to page until it is emptied
  (Escape empties it).

## The header

Beside the app's name, the header has the unit temperatures are shown in (°F, °C or K) and light or dark pages (or
the computer's own), both also under User Preferences. **Help** opens this documentation in a new tab, at the page
for the page you are on, and the version installed is beside it.

## Searching

Every search in the app matches the same way, as you type: each word typed, in any order and any case ("roof north"
finds "North roof"). User Preferences and System Settings have a filter at the top too, showing the sections whose
title, labels, help or choices have the words typed ("dark" finds Theme).
