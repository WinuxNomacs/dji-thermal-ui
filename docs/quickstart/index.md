# Quickstart

Six short tutorials, in order, from a first flight's images to its report. Each takes a few minutes, apart from the
waits while the app works; the pictures were made on a real flight of a roof (its place left out).

1. [Make a project and a flight](first-flight.md): the images uploaded and analyzed.
2. [Read the analysis](analysis.md): the flight's figures, its files and the problems found.
3. [Convert and open the mosaic](mosaic.md): a dataset of temperatures, stitched into mosaics.
4. [Measure and keep a finding](finding.md): a spot or an area measured, and kept with its frames.
5. [Print the reports](report.md): the flight's and the mosaic's, on paper or as PDF.
6. [Export](export.md): frames and mosaics as TIFF, PNG, R-JPEG or GeoTIFF.

Before you start, [install the app](../getting-started/install.md) and [run it](../getting-started/first-run.md).
